# NexTerm

NexTerm is a USB serial terminal for Android. Plug a serial adapter or a development board into your phone or tablet with an OTG cable, then read what it prints, send commands and watch the control lines.

It lists the adapters that are plugged in and has drivers for CDC-ACM, CH340/CH341, CP210x, FTDI and PL2303. It shows the traffic in three views, Console, Trace and Hex, sends text, raw bytes and saved commands, and handles the control lines DTR, RTS, CTS, DSR, CD and RI and BREAK. It is free software, released under the GNU General Public License v3.
